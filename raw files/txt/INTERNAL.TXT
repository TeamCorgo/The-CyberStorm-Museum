AntiGravity Unit
   Required Rank:    7
   Armor Factor:     100
   Weight:           0
   Cost:             7000
   Repair Factor:    50
   Energy to start:  20
   Energy per turn:  20
   Notes:   Makes effective gravity on Herc like a low-gravity world.
            This subtracts 125 points from the gravity, with a cap
            at 25% gravity.  
   Example: If you were on a 200% gravity world, the antigrav unit would 
            subtract 125 points from the gravity applied to the herc, so 
            that the herc would move as if on a 75% gravity world.   
   Example: If you were on a 80% gravity world, the antigrav device would
            subtract 125 points from the gravity applied to the herc
            (which would be -45% gravity).   But since that is below the
            effectiveness cap of the unit (the unit can only lower the
            gravity to a minimum of 25%), the resulting gravity applied 
            to the herc is the 25%.
   Example: If you were on a 25% gravity world, the antigravity device
            would have no effect whatsoever, since it is not capable of
            lowering the gravity any further.  You should turn off the 
            device, since it is needlessly consuming energy.
