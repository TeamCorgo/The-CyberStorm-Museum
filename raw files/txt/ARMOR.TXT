Name     = Armor Type name
Rank     = Required rank
Base     = Base Cost
Wt       = Weight
SWE      = Define special weapon effects (See table)
HEX      = Hex reinforcement

            N = Normal.  Balanced protection on all sides.
            R = Reinforced.  Heavier protection in front
                  Front:         Only 60% damage goes through
                  Front-Right:   Only 80% damage goes through
                  Front-Left:    Only 80% damage goes through
                  Back sides:    Damage is amplified by 50%

P100     = Percent of damage deflected when armor is functioning 100%
P0       = Percent of damage deflected when armor is functioning 0%
S100     = Amount of damage subtracted when armor is functioning 100%
S0       = Amount of damage subtracted when armor is functioning 0%
