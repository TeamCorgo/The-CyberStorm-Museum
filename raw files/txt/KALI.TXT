Playing CyberStorm using Kali (tm)
========================================================================

If you are a user of the Kali Internet gaming system, you may use it to
play multiplayer games of CyberStorm.  This document will give you the 
basic steps.  For further information, check out the site page at:

      http://www.techline.com/~outlaw/guns/cs/

Here you will find the latest information on multiplayer CyberStorm,
upcoming tournaments, mailing lists, downloads, and the status of the 
latest Windows 95 versions of Kali.

Instructions for Kali CyberStorm (For Host):
========================================================================

   1. Log onto Kali and find players. 
      Note: The official Kali Server is CyberStation
   2. Agree on a starting time. 
   3. Close Kali (optional) and open CyberStorm. 
   4. Load the force you wish to use. 
   5. Click Launch, and select Winsock TCP connection. 
   6. Generate new game. 
   7. Select parameters and wait for players to join. 



Instructions for Kali CyberStorm (For other Players):
========================================================================

   1. Log onto Kali and find players and host. 
      Note: The official Kali Server is CyberStation
   2. Use /whois command to locate the host's IP. 
   3. Run Kalidp.exe ***.***.**.** where the stars is the host's IP. 
   4. Close Kali. (optional) 
   5. Run CyberStorm. 
   6. Load a force and click launch. 
   7. Pick TCP connection. 
   8. Join the host's game. You may have to update once or twice. 
   9. Haggle over parameters and play.
