Type = Type of sensors
Rank = Required rank
AFac = Armor factor. This is a factor that says how much damage 
       applied to the component is passed through.  An Armor factor of
       40 means that only 40% of the damage is applied to the component,
       and that 60% is deflected by the components toughness.  An armor
       factor of 200 means that the damage is effectively doubled, 
       meaning the component is not very sturdy.
Wt   = Weight
Cost = Base cost
Enrg = Energy cost charged per turn
Max  = Max Range
LOS  = LOS (line-of-sight) ability description.  This describes
        the ability to see objects out of a units direct line of sight
        (over hills, in canyons, etc.)
