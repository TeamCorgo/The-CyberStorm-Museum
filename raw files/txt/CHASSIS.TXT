Type     = Chassis Type
Rk       = Required rank
AFac     = Armor factor.   This is a factor that says how much damage 
           applied to the component is passed through.  An Armor factor
           of 40 means that only 40% of the damage is applied to the 
           component, and that 60% is deflected by the components 
           toughness.  An armor factor of 200 means that the damage is
           effectively doubled, meaning the component is not very
           sturdy.
Weight   = Weight
Cost     = Base Cost
Jump     = Jump costs
Crch     = Crouch costs
