Shield

Shields offer the best protection against enemy cannon and missile fire. Each Herc has a different set of Shield classes available for defense. Each Shield is rated in three ways:

Maximum Shield Level – the Shield strength undamaged.

Regeneration Rate – the amount of power restored to the Shield Level after each turn, assuming a perfect operating condition. (Shields can be damaged by incoming fire.)

Energy Used Per Turn – the amount of reactor power required to set the Shields at the beginning of each turn.

Also notice that better Shields will negatively affect the Speed Rating of the Herc.
