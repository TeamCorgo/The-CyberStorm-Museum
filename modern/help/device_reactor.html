Reactor

Some reactors are more efficient than others. You will notice, while in the Herc Bay, that some reactors raise a Herc’s Speed Rating, while others lower it, even though every reactor upgrade increases the total amount of power available to a Herc.

Fission – reliably provides high levels of clean energy.

Ion – reactor output increased through less protection for the safer ion fuel.

Matter Conversion – power output is reduced through numerous safeguards. Nevertheless, this reactor is more efficient than the Ion reactor.
