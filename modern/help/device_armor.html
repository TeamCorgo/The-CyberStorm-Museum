Armor

Standard – this is the cheapest armor available.

Trinnium – more effective than Standard armor, particularly against cannon and missile attacks.

Assault – this is Trinnium armor that is better designed to protect against frontal attacks; rear protection does suffer.

Didrate – more effective than Trinnium armor, particularly against plasma-based attacks.

Tri-di – more effective than Didrate armor; also particularly effective against plasma-based attacks.

Improved 8.6cm Assault – this is Tri-di armor that is better designed to protect against frontal attacks; rear protection does suffer.

Antiballistic Coat – allows extreme agility and good protection against cannon attacks.

Advanced Heat Differential – more effective than Didrate armor; also allows extreme agility and good protection against plasma attacks.

Crystalline Mesh – diffuses laser-based attacks extremely well. Much less effective against other attacks.

Liquid Tri – more effective than Tri-di armor.

Heavy Assault – weighted Liquid Tri armor; better designed to protect against frontal attacks; rear protection does suffer.

Clearplast – extremely effective against all attacks except lasers.

Numbers indicate armor rating, based on the deflective and ablative capabilities of the armor; a blank slot means the Herc is not capable of carrying that armor.
