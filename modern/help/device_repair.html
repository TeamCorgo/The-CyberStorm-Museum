Repair

All Repair Devices are gradually available to all Hercs.

There are two ways to repair your Hercs: under Manage Hercs in the Herc Bay, or while on a mission if you have the proper technology on board. This technology comes in several designs and is accessed in the Damage Model during missions. (See Making Repairs.)

ProtoNanite Repair – an integration of rudimentary nanoprinciples with existing self-repair technology; 150%.

Partial Nanorepair – improvements in nanotechnology broaden the repair potential; 250%.

Enhanced Nanorepair – full system distribution with mechanical support increases regenerative capabilities; 350%.

True Nanorepair – Nanite conditioning and self-specialization allows the first nano-only repair system; 400%.

Nanorepair Glands – continued sophistication of nanotechnology enables a gland-like limited production system to increase repair potential; 450%.
