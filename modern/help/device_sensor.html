Sensor

Standard – enhances image detection with increased magnification and multi-particle detection; this category includes all Standard Sensors, the Crystal Matrix and the Advanced Crystal Matrix Sensors.

Scout – advanced equipment with the Sensei or Shadow’s signal-dampened chassis allows far greater detection; this category includes all Scout and Scout Crystal Matrix Sensors.

Each Sensor is rated three ways:

Range Maximum – the maximum number of hexes of visibility the system offers.

Indirect Visibility Capability – the overall ability of the sensor to see objects that are not in direct line of sight. P = Poor; G = Good.

Energy Used Per Turn – the amount of reactor power required to set the Sensor at the beginning of each turn.
