Bioderm Rank

Bioderms have 25 alpha-numeric classifications through which they progress as Herc pilots. They are:

A-1, A-2, A-3 … E-5. Each increase in Bioderm rank adds one point to each of the following: Health, Stability, Toxin threshold, and one point to each skill rating.
